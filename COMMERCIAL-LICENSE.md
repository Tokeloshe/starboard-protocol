Under the AGPL, if you incorporate this code into flight software, ground
software, or a network service, you must make the complete corresponding source
of your combined work available to its users under the same license.

**Most satellite operators cannot and will not open their proprietary flight or
ground software.** For those users, a separate commercial license is available
that waives the AGPL's copyleft obligations.

A commercial license grants:

- The right to link and ship Starboard in closed-source flight/ground software.

The commercial license concerns only this repository's *reference source code*.
